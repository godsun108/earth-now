# THEN

THEN is EARTH NOW's temporal interface over recorded observations.

PLAY EARTH advances the existing seven-day observation window hour by hour. It does not interpolate, reconstruct, or invent states between observations. A point appears only when its recorded timestamp enters the selected window.

Future archive-backed replay will load historical ATLAS snapshots directly. The invariant remains: historical playback must distinguish recorded observation, calculated state, and forecast data.
